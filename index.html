About

I'm a sports journalist and graduate student at Northwestern University's Medill School of Journalism. My work focuses on the intersection of sports, community, human rights, and culture.

I'm drawn to stories that go beyond the game—exploring how sports reflect and shape our communities. Most specifically I'm interested in the social justice movements within womens sports, from grassroots organizing in the leagues, to the fan discourse outside of it.

Beat interests: Women's sports • Sports business • Community impact • Investigative reporting
